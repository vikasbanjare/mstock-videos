## 1. Lens and spine

- **Obvious lens:** "Now you can buy US stocks from India." Every competitor says it.
- **Lens used:** the door was never locked by *distance*, *price* and *paperwork*. Those are the three reasons US stocks always felt like somebody else's game, and the page answers each one with a fact.
- **Spine (kept from the built film):** 0 → door → key → light → burst. The ending is the same last dab: **Customer by habit. Shareholder by choice.** It loops back into SCROLL.
- **Dance (but / therefore):**
  1. Your whole day runs on American companies.
  2. **BUT** how many do you own? (0)
  3. **THEREFORE** it always felt out of reach: too far, too expensive, too complicated.
  4. **BUT** not anymore. We opened the door.
  5. **THEREFORE** US stocks, now on m.Stock: 5,000+ names, buy from India.
  6. **BUT** what does a share cost? Own a piece, from $1.
  7. **BUT** how hard is it? 100% digital account opening.
  8. **THEREFORE** customer by habit, shareholder by choice.

## 5. Left out on purpose

| Page claim | Why it stays out of 30 s |
|---|---|
| "Free account", "₹0" | The page itself shows "Open Free\* Account" and "0.25% brokerage\*". An asterisk can't be spoken. |
| "No paperwork", "no branch visits" | FAQ 6 says that for KYC. FAQ 5 says SBI, PSU and some other banks may need Form A2 at a branch to fund. So the script says *account opening* only. |
| "Start investing in minutes" | The page says sign-up takes minutes, but funding is T+2 or more depending on the bank. |
| "No hidden charges", "SIPC protection up to USD 500,000", "US-regulated custody" | Regulatory and protection claims. Leave to the page and compliance. |
| Apple, Microsoft, Amazon, Tesla, NVIDIA | The page names them. The film keeps painted generic cards until legal clears the names. |
| LRS, GIFT City, ViewTrade, tax, order types, withdrawal rules | Landing-page material, not 30 s material. |
| "Every Indian can invest" | Eligibility: Resident Indians via LRS up to USD 250,000 per PAN per year; NRIs except those based in the US or Canada. |

## 7. Issues on the page itself (not in the script)

- **Capital-gains contradiction.** The tax section says long-term gains (24 months or more) are 12.5% "with no indexation benefits". FAQ 3 says 12.5% "with indexation". One is wrong. [Likely] the FAQ is the wrong one, but tax or compliance must confirm. The film will drive traffic to this page.
- **The page never says MAPS.** It is branded m.Stock throughout. The end card shows the MAPS International Markets logo. Confirm that MAPS is a customer-facing name before the film runs.
