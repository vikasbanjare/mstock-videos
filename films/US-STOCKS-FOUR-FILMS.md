# m.Stock · US Stocks · four films for all of India

Built on the thinking behind CRED's "The 21 million club": **one true fact → made physical → one subject carried through
real worlds while the scale grows → the fact revealed at the end in small type → logo.** We borrow the thinking, never
their shots (no card ribbon, no astronaut, no space-walk).

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps master (cut-downs later), about 35 s each, no voice-over, no subtitles.
- **Shots:** AI-generated video (text-to-video), 5 s per shot, generated 1–2 s longer than used. Type, logo, legal
  footer, music and sound are added in the edit (never asked of the video model).
- **Sound:** the music bed and sound design already approved in the light film, re-cut per film.
- **Type:** small tracked capitals, Open Sans 600, white at 90%, one line per beat, held ≥ 1.5 s.
- **End card:** black, m.Stock logo (dark version), "US stocks, now on m.Stock", legal footer, 2 s still.

## Shared style block (paste at the end of every prompt)

> Photoreal cinematic film still in motion, shot on a large-format digital cinema camera with anamorphic lenses,
> natural motivated light, gentle film grain, shallow depth of field, rich but restrained colour, deep navy shadows and
> warm amber highlights, one smooth camera move, no cuts, real locations in India unless stated, ordinary Indian people
> in everyday clothes, no text, no letters, no signage, no logos, no brand names, no watermark.

**Negative prompt (every shot):** text, letters, numbers, subtitles, captions, signage, logos, brand names, watermark,
user interface, readable screens, distorted faces, extra fingers, warped architecture, flicker, cartoon, CGI look.

**Phone screens** are always generated blank and glowing; the m.Stock screen is composited in the edit.

**Recommended model:** Kling 3.0 on Higgsfield (8.75 credits per 5 s shot, measured) or any tool you prefer. Generate
2 takes per shot and pick by continuity: the light direction and colour must match the next shot.
